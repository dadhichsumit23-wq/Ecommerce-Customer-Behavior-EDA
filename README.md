# 🛒 E-Commerce Customer Behavior Analysis (EDA)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on an E-commerce Customer Behavior dataset to understand customer purchasing patterns, engagement, lifetime value, and churn behavior. The analysis aims to uncover actionable business insights that can help improve customer retention, increase sales, and support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

E-commerce businesses generate large volumes of customer data, but understanding customer behavior is challenging. This project analyzes customer demographics, purchasing habits, engagement metrics, and churn-related factors to identify key patterns and provide business recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze customer demographics and purchasing behavior.
* Identify the most valuable customer segments.
* Explore factors affecting customer lifetime value.
* Understand customer engagement patterns.
* Analyze churn-related behavior.
* Generate actionable business insights and recommendations.

---

## 📊 Dataset Information

* **Dataset:** E-commerce Customer Behavior Dataset
* **Rows:** 50,000
* **Columns:** 25
* **Source:** Kaggle

## 🧹 Data Cleaning

* Checked for duplicate records.
* Verified data types.

---

## 📈 Exploratory Data Analysis

The project answers 25 business-oriented questions, including:

* Customer demographics analysis
* Purchasing behavior analysis
* Customer engagement analysis
* Customer lifetime value analysis
* Churn analysis
* Correlation analysis

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

* Highly engaged customers tend to make more purchases.
* Long-term members generally contribute higher lifetime value.
* Average order value positively influences customer lifetime value.
* Customer behavior varies across different countries and cities.
* Higher return rates and customer service interactions may increase churn risk.
* Mobile app usage and customer engagement show a positive relationship.

---

## 📌 Business Recommendations

* Focus marketing campaigns on high-value customer segments.
* Improve customer retention through loyalty programs.
* Reduce cart abandonment with personalized reminders.
* Enhance customer support to minimize churn.
* Increase customer engagement through mobile applications and email campaigns.
* Continuously monitor customer behavior for data-driven decision-making.

---

## 📋 Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Exploratory Data Analysis can transform raw customer data into meaningful business insights. The findings help identify customer behavior patterns, improve retention strategies, and support better business decisions for long-term growth.
